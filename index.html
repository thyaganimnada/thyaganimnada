About Me

Who am I?

I'm a passionate Software Developer from Sri Lanka, currently pursuing a Higher Diploma in Computing and Software Engineering at ICBT Campus.

I enjoy designing and developing practical software solutions, including Android applications, web applications, and desktop-based systems. My technical experience includes Kotlin, Java, PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and modern development tools.

I have worked on several academic and personal projects, including MediCare LK, a healthcare mobile application; SwiftDrive, a web-based car rental management system; and GadgetGalaxy, a Java-based desktop application developed using Object-Oriented Programming principles.

I have also developed and published Key Touch, an Android application available on Google Play under the N.Dev brand. This project reflects my interest in building useful mobile applications and improving everyday digital experiences.

My main interests include Android development, web technologies, database management, and software engineering. I am always eager to learn new technologies, improve my programming skills, and create innovative solutions to real-world problems.
